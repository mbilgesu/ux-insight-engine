# Moderator notes — mobile banking prototype, March 2026 cohort

Participant P04 hesitated on the main menu; could not locate transfers without help — clear navigation issue.
P04 said the success toast disappeared too fast and they were not sure the transfer actually posted.
The loading skeleton on the account list never resolved for several seconds; user assumed the app froze.
Participant described the legal disclaimer wall as intimidating and wondered if the app was official.
P05 struggled with the date picker: validation only said invalid date without explaining the required format.
P05 tried to undo a mistaken recipient selection but there was no obvious way to go back one step.
Participant missed the primary call to action because it looked like secondary grey text on a busy dashboard.
P06 reported that after a network error the form cleared and they had to re-enter all beneficiary details.
P06 said terminology for pending versus processing transactions did not match their mental model from other banks.
Participant noted the biometric login prompt felt flaky; sometimes it failed silently with no explanation.
P07 abandoned the flow when the CAPTCHA appeared twice in a row without any message about why.
P07 commented that the help link opened a generic FAQ instead of context-sensitive guidance for transfers.
